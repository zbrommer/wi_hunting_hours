Privacy

WI Shooting Hours (wishootinghours.com and its apps) · Updated November 25, 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no cookies, and nothing you enter is sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (hunt type, area, zone, town, and whether alerts are on) are saved only on your device, in the browser’s local storage. Clearing site data or uninstalling removes them.
  • Your location, if you tap “Use my location,” is used on your device to pick your zone and area. It is never sent anywhere or stored.
  • Alerts are created by your device. No server sends them, and we don’t collect any notification token.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records the page viewed, the referring site, browser and screen size, and an approximate country. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not track you across sites. We only see totals.

Other services the page loads

  • Google Fonts supplies the page’s typefaces. Your device requests them from Google, which receives your IP address as part of any web request (Google’s privacy policy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs (GitHub’s privacy statement).

Children

The site isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
