Times come from the DNR's __YEAR__ Northern and Southern Area shooting-hours charts: deer, bear, small game and fall turkey (30 minutes before sunrise to 20 minutes after sunset), and waterfowl and spring turkey (30 minutes before sunrise to sunset), with each zone's added minutes applied. They're local clock time, daylight saving included. Zones are set by longitude; town lookup uses ZIP-code town centers, so it's approximate — if you hunt near a line, check the official DNR charts and zone map. Some seasons and opening days have their own special hours. Always confirm season dates and rules in the current regulations. Visits are counted with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data.
