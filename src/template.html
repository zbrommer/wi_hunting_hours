Times come from the DNR's __YEAR__ Northern and Southern Area shooting-hours charts for deer, bear, small game and fall turkey (30 minutes before sunrise to 20 minutes after sunset), with each zone's added minutes applied. They're local clock time, daylight saving included. Zones are set by longitude; town lookup uses ZIP-code town centers, so it's approximate — if you hunt near a line, check the official DNR charts and zone map. Waterfowl and spring turkey use different hours. Always confirm season dates and rules in the current regulations.
